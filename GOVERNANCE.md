# Governance

Polityra CMS is maintained by the Polityra organization. The project is
developed in the open, and issues and pull requests are the primary venues for
proposing changes and discussing project work.

## Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for the project's direction, releases, repository
administration, and enforcement of project policies. They review contributions
for usefulness, correctness, maintainability, security, and consistency with
the project goals described in the [README](./README.md).

## Decisions

Contributors are encouraged to discuss substantial changes before
implementation. Maintainers make final decisions when consensus is not
practical, explaining the decision in the relevant issue or pull request when
possible. Project policies may be revised through a documented proposal and
maintainer review.

## Participation

Contributions are subject to the [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and
the [contribution guidance](./CONTRIBUTING.md). Maintainers may close,
request changes to, or decline contributions that do not meet project
requirements or policies.
