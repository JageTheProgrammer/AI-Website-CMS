# Code of Conduct

Polityra CMS welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds,
experience levels, and perspectives. We expect everyone in project spaces to
help keep them respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking or demeaning people.
- Make room for differing viewpoints and assume good intent while addressing
  the impact of what was said or done.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information or
  private communications without their permission.
- Follow the project policies and the rules of the platforms where the project
  is hosted.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained personal attacks, sexualized
attention without consent, doxxing, and deliberate disruption of project
spaces are not acceptable. This applies to project discussions, code review,
issue trackers, and other spaces used for project work.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
repository's private contact channels. Do not use a public issue to disclose
sensitive details. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect the
reporter's confidentiality as far as practical, and may take proportionate
action, including a warning, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent
restriction from project spaces.

Maintainers are expected to apply these rules consistently and to explain
enforcement decisions when doing so does not compromise someone's privacy or
safety.
